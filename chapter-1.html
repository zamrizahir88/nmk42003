Basics

Before measuring anything, it helps to see the big picture: where measurement fits in modern connected systems, and what instrumentation actually is.

    Classification of instruments

    Instruments can be classified by their working principle, the technique they use to produce a result, the type of signal they produce, and their function.

      Performance characteristics

      Performance characteristics tell you how good an instrument is, and help you choose the right one. They come in two kinds: static (for steady quantities) and dynamic (for changing ones).

        Standards

        Every measurement is a comparison. Standards are the agreed references that make a volt measured in Perlis the same as a volt measured anywhere else.

        Measurement errors

        Every measurement has some error. This section shows how to put a number on it, how instrument specifications turn into errors, how to round results honestly, and where errors come from.

          Statistical analysis

          Random errors can't be removed one reading at a time, but many readings together tell you both the best value and how much to trust it.
