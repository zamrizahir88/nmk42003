Digital to Analog Converter (DAC)

A DAC turns a binary number into an analog voltage. There are two types: the binary-weighted resistor DAC and the R-2R ladder.

    Analog to Digital Converter (ADC)

    An ADC measures a voltage and turns it into a binary number.
