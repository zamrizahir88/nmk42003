Lab 3 · Virtual lab

Smart Sensors

An RFID Card Reader with an LCD Display

Plan and rehearse the whole of Lab 3 here before you come to the lab. You'll read RFID cards with a phone and with an RC522 reader, show text on an LCD, and join the two into a standalone card reader.

Objectives

  • obtain the UID of an RFID card or tag with an NFC-enabled mobile phone
  • interface the ESP32 to the RFID RC522 module
  • connect the ESP32 to a 16 × 4 LCD through an I2C I/O expander
  • assemble a standalone ESP32-based RFID card reader

This is a simulation: nothing here talks to a real board or a real card. The virtual cards on this device have their own UIDs, different from everyone else's. In the lab you'll do the same steps on the real hardware, with your own cards.

Before You Start

The equipment, the order of the work, and how an RFID card is read.

    Lesson 1: Read a Card with a Phone

    Use an NFC phone to read a card's type and serial number, the UID.

    Lesson 2: The RC522 Reader

    Wire the RC522 to the ESP32, and read the same UID with a program of your own.

      Lesson 3: The I2C LCD

      Connect a 16 × 4 LCD with four wires, and show your name and matric number on it.

        Lesson 4: The RFID Display System

        Merge the two programs into one: a standalone reader that shows each card's UID on the LCD.
