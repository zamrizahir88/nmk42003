Chapter 7

Transducer Calibration

By the end of this chapter you can

  • explain what calibration is and why sensors must be calibrated
  • follow the five steps of a calibration: reference, table, fit, program, check
  • fit a straight line (or a curve) to calibration data and use it in an ESP32 sketch
  • explain why the whole measuring system is calibrated, not just the sensor

Try the virtual lab: record readings, fit the line and watch the ESP32 show real units. Tap Show on any panel to see the working. The exercises at the end check what you have learnt.
