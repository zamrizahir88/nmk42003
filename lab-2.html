Transduction and Conversion Calibration

Analog Angular Sensor Calibration and Wireless Display

Plan and rehearse the whole of Lab 2 here before you come to the lab. You'll turn a potentiometer into an angle sensor: power it safely, read it with the ESP32, calibrate it against a protractor, and show the angle on a phone and on a web page.

Objectives

  • obtain the calibration data of an angular sensor: the angle and the ADC count
  • plot the angle against the ADC count and fit a first-order polynomial (a straight line)
  • display the calibrated angle on a mobile phone and in a web browser

This is a simulation: nothing here talks to a real board. The virtual potentiometer on this device is a little different from everyone else's, so your m and c are your own. In the lab you'll do the same steps on the real ESP32, and your real numbers will be different again.
