Introduction

Transducers convert one form of energy into another. Sensors bring the physical world into a system; actuators act on it.

            Home Exercise: Submit on URLearn

            Work these out on paper using the methods on this page, then submit your answers on URLearn. The answers are not given here.

            1. A potentiometer has a total resistance of 10 kΩ. The wiper can move between the Vin terminal and the GND terminal. Assume Vin = 5 V.
              • If the wiper is exactly at the midpoint, what is the resistance between Vin and the wiper (R1), and between the wiper and GND (R2)? What is Vout at this midpoint?
              • If the wiper is closer to GND, so that R2 = 1 kΩ, what are R1 and Vout?
            2. A thermistor used as a temperature sensor has a length of 2 m and a cross-sectional area of 0.0005 m². The resistivity of the material is 0.01 Ω·m.
              • Calculate its resistance.
              • If the length is doubled with the same cross-sectional area, what is the new resistance?
            3. A capacitive sensor has two parallel plates of area A = 0.02 m², separated by d = 0.001 m, with a dielectric of relative permittivity εr = 4. Calculate its capacitance.
            4. A capacitor has plates of total length L = 10 cm and width w = 5 cm, separated by d = 1 mm (ε0 = 8.854 × 10−12 F/m, εwater = 80, εair = 1). If the plates are submerged 3 cm in water (x = 3 cm) and the rest is in air, calculate the net capacitance.
            5. A simple inductor coil has N = 100 turns, cross-sectional area A = 0.005 m², length l = 0.2 m and a core of relative permeability μr = 200 (μ0 = 4π × 10−7 H/m). Calculate its inductance.
